Airgap

A local-first data governance & PII lineage auditor for DataHub.
The problem

PII is tagged at the source.
Then lineage forgets.

  • Classified data flows downstream — and loses its classification.
  • It lands untagged, in assets nobody owns.
  • Automate the audit with a cloud LLM, and you leak the very data you protect.

🔴 CRITICAL · AIRGAP-R1-002

Untagged PII propagation into marketing_enriched

users.email → stg_users.email → dim_customer.email → marketing_enriched.email ⚠ untagged
Email & name reach a marketing dataset with no PII classification, exposed to a Looker dashboard — a GDPR risk. Explained by a local model.
Fix: propagate the PII tag & glossary term to the leaked fields, or mask before this asset.
The leak map
Red is where PII leaks. Ranked by blast radius — which reach BI dashboards.
Remediation · ready for a PR
# Airgap-generated DataHub SDK fix — AIRGAP-R1-002
for field in ["email", "name"]:
    field_urn = make_schema_field_urn(DATASET_URN, field)
    emitter.emit(MetadataChangeProposalWrapper(
        entityUrn=field_urn,
        aspect=GlobalTagsClass(tags=[TagAssociationClass(tag=PII_TAG)]),
    ))
